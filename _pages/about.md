I am a data scientist and software developer with a PhD in particle physics from the University of Manchester. My doctoral research focused on neutrino detection using liquid argon time projection chambers (LArTPCs), with an emphasis on improving the precision and scope of solar neutrino measurements through advances in detector technology and data analysis. I also hold an MSc in Physics from University College London and a BSc in Physics with Astronomy from the University of São Paulo.

Before my PhD, I worked at Amazon in London as a Business Analyst. My work included analysing operational data and developing automated reporting and data-processing workflows. I contributed to the launch of new delivery stations across Europe and worked with teams across the business to solve operational problems. The role gave me experience applying analytical and programming skills in a fast-paced, professional environment.

I also build software projects in data analysis, automation, machine learning, and scientific computing. These projects allow me to explore computational methods outside formal research and develop software independently.

Beyond my technical work, aviation is my passion. I am a licensed private pilot in both Brazil and the United Kingdom, and I value the discipline, situational awareness, and procedural rigour that flying demands.
